How to get IP camera alerts on Telegram, with a photo

Want your phone to buzz with a photo when someone walks up to the gate? Local Vision detects the person on your own PC and sends the snapshot to your Telegram through a bot that only you control. Setup takes about 10 minutes.

What you need 1. Create the bot 2. Get the chat ID 3. Send a test 4. Turn on alerts per camera FAQ

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC that stays on where the cameras are. No GPU required.
  • IP cameras with RTSP or USB webcams (RTSP URLs by brand).
  • Telegram on your phone.
  • Local Vision installed. The trial is free for 14 days, no card required.

1. Create the bot with BotFather

  1. In Telegram, search for @BotFather (the official one has the blue check) and open the chat.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. Pick a name, such as “Home Cameras”, and a username ending in bot, such as home_cameras_bot.
  4. BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:AAH.... Copy it.

Keep the token private: anyone who has it can send messages through your bot.

2. Get the chat ID

  1. Open a chat with your new bot and send any message, like “hi”.
  2. In Local Vision, open the credentials screen and paste the token into the bot token field.
  3. Click the button to detect the chat ID automatically. The destination chat ID is filled in.
  4. Save.

Want alerts in a family group? Add the bot to the group, send a message there and detect the chat ID again.

3. Send a test

On the same screen, send a test message. It should reach your phone within seconds. If it doesn't, check the PC's internet connection and make sure you messaged the bot before detecting the chat ID.

4. Turn on alerts per camera

  • Enable Telegram alerts on each camera that deserves them. The alert carries the event photo and a short message.
  • Draw a zone over the gate, fence or pool so passers-by on the street don't trigger alerts.
  • Register household faces to tell family from strangers.
  • Set the repeat interval so one person standing in frame doesn't flood the chat.

FAQ

Does my camera video go to Telegram?

No. Detection runs on your computer and only the alert photo and text are sent to Telegram. Continuous video and recordings stay on the machine.

Does it work with Hikvision, Dahua, Reolink or Tapo cameras?

Yes, if the camera has RTSP, which covers most IP models from these brands. On Tapo, create the camera account in the Tapo app first to enable RTSP.

Do I pay per camera?

No. The subscription is per installation, with as many cameras as your computer can handle.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Detection and recording keep running on the computer. Telegram alerts need internet to be delivered.
