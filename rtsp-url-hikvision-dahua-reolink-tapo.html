RTSP URLs for Hikvision, Dahua, Amcrest, Reolink and Tapo cameras

RTSP is the camera's video address on your home network. Any NVR or AI software, Local Vision included, reads the image through it without going through the manufacturer's cloud. Here are the URL formats for the most common brands, plus NVR channels and troubleshooting.

The RTSP URL format

rtsp://username:password@CAMERA-IP:554/path

Port 554 is the default. The path changes by brand. Most cameras offer a main stream (full resolution) and a sub stream (lower resolution, lighter to process). In Local Vision you usually don't type the URL: pick the manufacturer, or leave it on automatic and the app tries the known paths. For any other brand, use the advanced option and paste the full URL.

Hikvision (and HiLook)

  • Main: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101
  • Sub: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/102

RTSP is enabled by default on most models. The password is the one set when the camera was activated.

Dahua and Amcrest

  • Main: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0
  • Sub: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=1

Many brands built on Dahua hardware, Amcrest included, use the same format.

Reolink

  • Main: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/h264Preview_01_main
  • Sub: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/h264Preview_01_sub

On some models RTSP must be enabled first in the Reolink app or web interface, under network or advanced port settings. Battery-powered and some Wi-Fi-only Reolink models do not offer RTSP. In Local Vision, paste the URL using the advanced option.

TP-Link Tapo (C200, C210, C310, C500 and others)

Tapo RTSP only works after you create a camera account, which is different from your TP-Link login: in the Tapo app, open the camera settings, go to advanced settings and then camera account, and create a username and password.

  • High quality: rtsp://username:password@IP:554/stream1
  • Low quality: rtsp://username:password@IP:554/stream2

Menu names may vary slightly between Tapo app versions.

NVR and DVR channels

For cameras plugged into a recorder, use the recorder's IP and change the channel:

  • Dahua and Amcrest: channel=2 for camera 2, channel=3 for camera 3, and so on.
  • Hikvision: 201 for camera 2 main stream, 301 for camera 3; a final digit of 2 means the sub stream.
  • Reolink NVR: h264Preview_02_main for channel 2, and so on.

Won't connect?

  • Test in VLC first: Media › Open Network Stream, paste the URL. If VLC can't play it, the URL or credentials are wrong.
  • Special characters in the password such as @, # or : break the URL. Use a password without them, or URL-encode them.
  • Check the IP in your router's device list; a DHCP reservation keeps it from changing.
  • Cloud-only cameras (many Ring, Arlo, Nest and Wyze models) don't expose RTSP at all.

FAQ

What is the default RTSP port?

554. Some cameras let you change it in their network settings; if so, use the new port in the URL.

What is the difference between the main stream and the sub stream?

The main stream has the full resolution for recording and viewing. The sub stream is a lower-resolution copy that uses less bandwidth and CPU, which is useful for live views on slower machines.

Do Ring, Arlo or Nest cameras support RTSP?

Most of their models do not expose a local RTSP stream, so they can't be used with local NVR or AI software. Check your model's specifications for RTSP or ONVIF support.

Can Local Vision use these URLs?

Yes. Pick the manufacturer and the app builds the URL, or paste any RTSP URL using the advanced option when adding the camera.
