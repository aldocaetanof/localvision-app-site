A simpler Frigate and Blue Iris alternative for Windows

Frigate and Blue Iris are great tools, and a lot of people run them happily. Local Vision is for people who want local AI person detection, face recognition and plate reading on a regular Windows PC, set up from a graphical app, without Docker, YAML files or a separate AI add-on. Here's an honest comparison.

Short version

  • Frigate: open source NVR with excellent object detection, typically run in Docker and configured in YAML, often alongside Home Assistant.
  • Blue Iris: mature Windows NVR software with deep recording controls; AI detection usually comes from a separate add-on.
  • Local Vision: a Windows app with AI built in (people, faces, plates and zones), set up through a graphical interface, with a monthly subscription per installation.

When Frigate is the better choice

  • You already run Home Assistant or a home server with Docker.
  • You're comfortable editing configuration files.
  • You want free, open source software and community support.

When Blue Iris is the better choice

  • You need advanced recording, PTZ and many-camera controls.
  • You prefer a one-time license and don't mind configuring an AI add-on.

When Local Vision fits

  • You want AI person detection, face recognition and plate reading working together out of the box.
  • You have a regular Windows PC and no GPU or accelerator.
  • You install cameras for clients and want an admin panel plus a read-only view for residents.
  • You want photo alerts on Telegram without writing automations.

FAQ

Is Local Vision free like Frigate?

No. Local Vision is a paid subscription at US$ 29 per month per installation, with a 14-day free trial and no per-camera fees. Frigate is free and open source.

Do I need Docker or Linux?

No. Local Vision is a regular Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) app with an installer.

Do I need a GPU or a Coral accelerator?

No. Detection runs on the CPU. Performance depends on your processor, the resolution and the number of cameras.

Can I keep my Blue Iris or Frigate setup?

Yes. Cameras usually accept several RTSP connections, so you can try Local Vision on the same cameras without removing your current NVR, as long as the camera supports the extra stream.
