Features

An AI camera that only calls you when it matters

Not just another camera app: Local Vision analyzes every event and separates routine from what deserves your attention.

01

Local face recognition

Register authorized people and tune the sensitivity. The system tells a known face from a stranger and cuts unnecessary alerts.

02

Drawable restricted zones

Draw polygons over the image: pool, wall, dog run. You get an alert only when a person or pet crosses the zone, not the whole street.

03

Alerts that respect you

Event photo in the History and, if you enable it, on Telegram. Configurable cooldowns avoid repeated alerts.

04

Painless multi-camera

RTSP or webcam, per-camera zoom/brightness/contrast, continuous recording with smart disk protection and automatic reconnection.

05

Watchful Agent

The Watchful Agent classifies risk signals directly on the CPU, including hidden faces and suspicious approaches.

06

Built for installers

Full admin panel plus a read-only view for residents. One license per installation, backup/restore and a Windows installer.

07

License plate reading

Reads the plates of vehicles coming in or out, logs them in the History and lets you search by plate later — all processed locally.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a GPU or fast internet?

No. Detection runs on the CPU. Performance depends on your computer, resolution and number of cameras. Internet is needed only for integrations you enable, the subscription and remote access.

Do my images go to external services?

Continuous video, recordings and the face database stay on your machine. If you enable Telegram, alert photos are sent to it.

Will it work with my current cameras?

If they speak RTSP (most IP cameras, including Hikvision, Dahua, Reolink, Amcrest and TP-Link Tapo) or are USB webcams, yes.

Do I pay per camera?

No. The subscription is US$ 29/month per installation, with as many cameras as your computer can handle.

Who processes my payment?

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles billing, taxes, invoices and payment support.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the paid period. See the refund policy.

Windows blocked the installer (SmartScreen)?

The installer isn't code-signed yet. On the SmartScreen screen, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. You can verify the file's SHA256 from the download email.
