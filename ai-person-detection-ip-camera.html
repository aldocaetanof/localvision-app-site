AI person detection for the IP cameras you already own

Motion detection alerts you for leaves, headlights, rain and the cat. After a week you mute it. AI person detection looks at what is in the frame and only alerts when a person enters an area you care about. Local Vision adds it to the cameras you already have, running on your own Windows PC with no per-camera fees.

Why motion detection fails

Classic motion detection compares pixels between frames. Any change is an event: trees in the wind, car headlights, rain, insects near the IR light, shadows moving with the sun. Dozens of false alerts a night train you to ignore the one that matters.

How AI person detection works

Local Vision runs an object detection model (YOLO) on the camera stream. It classifies what it sees, so a person and a dog are different classes, and a swaying branch is nothing at all. Detection runs on the CPU: no GPU and no cloud upload required.

Zones: alert only where it matters

Draw a polygon over each camera image and alerts fire only when a person enters it:

  • Gate and fence line: alert when someone steps onto the property, not when they walk by on the sidewalk.
  • Pool: alert when a person or pet enters the pool area.
  • Back door and garage: areas nobody should cross at 3 a.m.

People outside the zone still show on the live view, but they don't trigger alerts. A cooldown keeps one person standing in frame from sending dozens of messages.

Known faces

Register the people who live in the house and the system tells a known face from a stranger, cutting alerts for your own family. Face data is processed and stored on your machine.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC that stays on where the cameras are. No GPU required.
  • IP cameras with RTSP (most Hikvision, Dahua, Amcrest, Reolink and TP-Link Tapo models) or USB webcams. See the RTSP URL guide.
  • Optionally, Telegram for photo alerts on your phone.

FAQ

Can I add person detection to an old IP camera?

Yes, if the camera exposes an RTSP stream or is a USB webcam. The AI runs on your computer, not on the camera, so the camera model does not need any built-in smart features.

Will it confuse my dog or cat with a person?

People and dogs are separate classes in the AI model. Mistakes can still happen, especially with poor night images or distant subjects, but far less often than with pixel-based motion detection.

Does it work at night?

Yes, with the camera's infrared image. Accuracy depends on image quality, so a camera with good IR and sensible placement gives the best results.

How many cameras can I use?

There is no per-camera limit in the license. The limit is your computer's performance, which depends on the CPU, the resolution and the number of cameras.
